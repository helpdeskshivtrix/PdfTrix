Compare documents

Form builder

Drag on the page to place a field, then Apply to write it into the PDF. Date fields hold up to 10 characters.

E-signature

Click the page where the signature goes, then Apply. This is an electronic signature with a SHA-256 record of the document, not a certificate-based digital signature.
